# 2026-05-28 Props 32x32 v1

## Goal

Evaluate whether PixelLab `create_map_object` can produce usable gothic cemetery props for VibeCemetery.

## Result

The props direction is more promising than the 16x16 terrain test. Objects are 32x32 transparent PNGs and should be evaluated as props placed inside 48x48 canvases, not stretched to 48x48.

## Initial Assessment

Strong candidates:

- simple stone cross grave marker
- candle cluster on ash-brown soil
- skull pile with dark soil
- short rusted iron fence segment

Medium candidates:

- small broken gothic tombstone
- dark dirt grave mound with sparse grey moss
- small cracked stone urn

Weak candidate:

- dead bush with charcoal branches, likely too thin to read on the map

## Next Review Step

Create a mockup that places these 32x32 props on 48x48 transparent canvases over real VibeCemetery ground tiles or a screenshot crop. Compare against existing grave and decor assets before accepting anything.
